Privacy Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026

The short version. Ember is a notebook app that runs on your own Mac. Your notes are plain files in a folder you choose. We do not run servers that hold your notes, we do not collect analytics, and we do not sell or share anything. When you connect an AI or a Google account, Ember talks to that service directly from your Mac using your own account, and the data goes only where you point it.

What Ember stores

  • Your notes, canvases, and settings live on your Mac, in your vault folder and in your Mac's own Application Support folder.
  • Ember does not create an account for you and does not send your notes to us. There is no Ember server that receives them.

AI providers you connect

  • Ember can use an AI account that you bring (for example Anthropic, Google, or OpenAI) or a model running on your own machine.
  • When you ask Ember a question, the text needed to answer it is sent from your Mac to the provider you chose, under your own account and that provider's privacy policy. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Your keys and sign-ins stay on your Mac (in the macOS Keychain or in files only your user can read).

Google Calendar and Google Tasks

If you connect a Google account, Ember uses Google's official sign-in to read and update the calendars and task lists in that account so that your schedule and to-dos can appear in your notebook.

  • Ember only requests the Calendar and Tasks permissions it needs for that job.
  • The data Google returns is used on your Mac to show and update your own events and tasks. It is not stored on any server of ours, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone.
  • Ember's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can disconnect at any time by removing Ember from your Google account's third-party access page, or by deleting the saved sign-in on your Mac. Disconnecting stops all access immediately.

Updates

Ember checks one small text file on this website to learn whether a newer version exists. That check sends no personal information.

Children

Ember is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
