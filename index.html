01The terminal

A full terminal, right in your notebook.

Run real commands and the command-line AI tools you already use, next to your notes. No second app, no window to switch to. Drag a note or a file straight into the terminal and keep going.

02Bring your own AI

Pick your AI. Your notes stay yours.

Sign in with Claude, ChatGPT, or Google. No keys, no setup. Switch your AI any time in Settings. Or stay on your own Mac with the free Ollama app, even with the Wi-Fi off. The Ollama app reads and answers; changing a note takes Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Notes the AI reads go to that AI over the internet. With the free Ollama app, no note leaves your Mac.

04It is all just files

The notes the AI reads are files you own.

Your notes are plain Markdown files on your Mac, and so is every chat. Open them in any editor, back them up, or read them ten years from now. What Ember's chat is told about you is one note, About You, that you can read and change. Walk away whenever you want. It was always yours.
