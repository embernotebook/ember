# Report a problem

Thank you for taking the time. A clear report gets fixed sooner.

## Before you write

- Ember offers each update itself. If one is waiting, take it first, then see if the problem is still there.
- Your Ember version is in Settings.

## Open an issue

1. Go to [New issue](https://github.com/embernotebook/ember/issues/new/choose) and pick **Something is not working**.
2. Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
3. Add your Ember version, your macOS version (Apple menu, then About This Mac) and which Mac you have.
4. A screenshot helps.

Please never put anything private in an issue: not your notes, not a key, not a password. Issues are public.

## Rather write?

Email [embernotebook@outlook.com](mailto:embernotebook@outlook.com) with the same details. Write this way too for anything private, or for a security problem.

## Your notes

Your notes are plain files on your Mac, and fixing a problem never needs them. The [privacy page](https://embernotebook.com/privacy.html) says what leaves your Mac and when.
